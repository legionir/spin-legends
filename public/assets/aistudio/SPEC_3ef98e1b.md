\# SPIN LEGENDS

\## Product Specification (SPEC)



\*\*Document Version:\*\* 1.0  

\*\*Status:\*\* Initial Specification  

\*\*Product Type:\*\* 3D Arena-Based Competitive Action Game  

\*\*Target Platforms:\*\* Windows PC and Android  

\*\*Primary Audience:\*\* Players interested in customization, competitive battles, collection, progression, and strategic gameplay.



\---



\# 1. Product Overview



\## 1.1. Product Vision



Spin Legends is a 3D competitive arena game centered around customizable mechanical spinning tops with unique combat characteristics, elemental powers, special abilities, and evolutionary progression.



Players build their own spinning tops using interchangeable mechanical components, develop their abilities, discover new components, and compete against increasingly challenging opponents in physics-driven battles.



Each spinning top has its own identity, characteristics, combat behavior, and progression path.



The game combines real-time physical combat with strategic player decisions. Spinning tops move, rotate, collide, and react to their environment autonomously, while players influence battles through tactical commands, combat modes, and special abilities.



The ultimate goal is to create a competitive experience where equipment configuration, strategic decisions, timing, and mastery determine the outcome of battles.



\## 1.2. Core Product Pillars



The product is built around seven fundamental pillars:



1\. \*\*Customization:\*\* Players can construct unique spinning tops using interchangeable components.

2\. \*\*Physics-Driven Combat:\*\* Movement, rotation, collisions, momentum, and stability directly influence battle outcomes.

3\. \*\*Strategic Gameplay:\*\* Players make meaningful decisions during real-time battles.

4\. \*\*Elemental Abilities:\*\* Each spinning top can possess unique elemental properties and special powers.

5\. \*\*Progression and Evolution:\*\* Spinning tops develop through experience, upgrades, and evolutionary transformations.

6\. \*\*Collection:\*\* Players discover, unlock, collect, and customize different spinning tops and components.

7\. \*\*Competitive Experience:\*\* Players face increasingly challenging opponents through multiple game modes.



\## 1.3. Product Objectives



The product must:



\- Deliver an engaging and visually impressive 3D battle experience.

\- Support meaningful customization and equipment combinations.

\- Provide distinct gameplay behaviors between different spinning top configurations.

\- Make strategic decisions relevant throughout battles.

\- Support long-term progression without making earlier equipment obsolete.

\- Provide an extensible foundation for future content and multiplayer functionality.

\- Run efficiently on both desktop and mobile hardware.

\# 3. Game Structure



\## 3.1. Arena-Based Gameplay



The game takes place in a collection of dedicated 3D battle arenas.



Players do not explore a continuous open world.



Instead, the experience is organized around:



\- A central game interface.

\- A spinning top collection system.

\- A customization workshop.

\- Dedicated battle arenas.

\- Competitive tournaments.

\- Training environments.

\- Progression and reward systems.



Each arena provides a dedicated environment with its own visual identity, geometry, physical characteristics, and optional environmental mechanics.

\# 4. Core Gameplay Requirements



\## 4.1. Main Gameplay Loop



The primary gameplay cycle consists of:



1\. Select or construct a spinning top.

2\. Configure mechanical components and abilities.

3\. Select a battle mode or challenge.

4\. Enter a battle arena.

5\. Launch the spinning top.

6\. Observe autonomous movement and physical interactions.

7\. Issue tactical commands and activate combat abilities.

8\. Defeat the opponent.

9\. Receive experience, currency, and equipment rewards.

10\. Upgrade, evolve, or modify the spinning top.

11\. Enter more challenging battles.



The progression loop must continuously introduce new customization and strategic possibilities.



\## 4.2. Battle Philosophy



Combat is real-time and physics-driven.



Spinning tops must operate as independent physical entities rather than animated objects following predetermined paths.



The player does not directly control every movement.



Instead, the player influences the spinning top through strategic commands, combat modes, and active abilities.



The combat system must balance:



\- Physical simulation.

\- Autonomous movement.

\- Tactical decision-making.

\- Equipment characteristics.

\- Resource management.

\- Ability timing.

\- Environmental interactions.



Player decisions must have a meaningful influence on battle outcomes.

\# 5. Spinning Top System



\## 5.1. Modular Construction



Each spinning top must consist of interchangeable mechanical components.



The initial component categories are:



| Component | Primary Responsibility |

|---|---|

| Spirit Core | Identity, elemental affinity, and core characteristics |

| Attack Blade | Collision geometry and offensive performance |

| Armor Ring | Protection and structural resistance |

| Weight Disc | Mass distribution and rotational characteristics |

| Performance Driver | Ground contact, movement, and stability |

| Energy Module | Energy capacity and ability performance |



Components must be independently configurable and visually represented in the assembled spinning top.



Changing a component must affect the resulting attributes and, where applicable, physical behavior.



\## 5.2. Spinning Top Attributes



Every spinning top must support the following attributes:



| Attribute | Description |

|---|---|

| Attack | Offensive effectiveness during collisions |

| Defense | Resistance to incoming impacts |

| Stamina | Available rotational endurance |

| Speed | Movement and acceleration capabilities |

| Weight | Physical mass |

| Stability | Resistance to losing balance |

| Control | Responsiveness to movement decisions |

| Energy | Capacity for activating special abilities |

| Critical | Potential for enhanced impact events |



Attributes must be derived from component characteristics, core properties, upgrades, and applicable progression bonuses.



\## 5.3. Combat Archetypes



The initial archetypes are:



\- Attacker

\- Defender

\- Balance

\- Stamina



Archetypes define general performance tendencies rather than strict equipment restrictions.



Players must be able to create unconventional combinations by mixing components from different combat styles.



\## 5.4. Customization Requirements



Players must be able to:



\- Equip and remove components.

\- Compare component attributes.

\- Preview assembled configurations.

\- Inspect spinning tops from multiple camera angles.

\- Change cosmetic appearance.

\- Save multiple configurations.

\- View the impact of equipment changes.

\- Restore previously saved configurations.



The system must prevent invalid component combinations.

\# 7. Combat System



\## 7.1. Combat Architecture Requirements



Combat must be:



\- Real-time.

\- Physics-driven.

\- Autonomous at the movement level.

\- Strategically controlled by the player.

\- Responsive to changing battle conditions.



The combat system must operate independently from menus and presentation logic.



\## 7.2. Battle Phases



Every standard battle must include:



1\. Preparation

2\. Launch

3\. Combat

4\. Critical

5\. Finish

6\. Rewards



Each phase must have clearly defined entry and exit conditions.



\## 7.3. Tactical Commands



Players must be able to issue strategic commands during combat.



The initial command set includes:



| Command | Description |

|---|---|

| Aggressive | Prioritize offensive engagement |

| Defensive | Prioritize protection and stability |

| Chase | Pursue the opponent |

| Circle | Maintain circular movement around the opponent |

| Evade | Avoid incoming collisions |

| Hold Position | Maintain a designated strategic area |

| Counter | Prepare defensive responses and counterattacks |

| Conserve Energy | Reduce unnecessary energy consumption |



The system must support configurable command durations, priorities, and cooldowns.



Commands must influence autonomous movement and decision-making rather than directly replacing physical simulation.



\## 7.4. Battle Modes



The initial battle mode system must include:



| Mode | Primary Effect |

|---|---|

| Attack Mode | Prioritizes offensive performance |

| Defense Mode | Prioritizes protection and stability |

| Speed Mode | Prioritizes acceleration and mobility |

| Stamina Mode | Prioritizes rotational endurance |

| Counter Mode | Prioritizes defensive reactions |

| Berserk Mode | Temporarily increases offensive performance with additional risks |



Battle modes must have identifiable gameplay consequences.



Mode activation may consume resources or introduce temporary limitations.



\## 7.5. Active Abilities



Each spinning top must support four ability slots:



| Slot | Category |

|---|---|

| 1 | Elemental Ability |

| 2 | Defensive Ability |

| 3 | Tactical Ability |

| 4 | Ultimate Ability |



Abilities must support:



\- Energy consumption.

\- Cooldowns.

\- Activation conditions.

\- Duration.

\- Effect strength.

\- Visual effects.

\- Audio effects.

\- Target selection where applicable.



The ability system must be extensible and data-driven.



\## 7.6. Resonance System



Resonance is a special combat resource used to activate ultimate abilities.



Resonance must increase through meaningful combat actions, including:



\- Successful attacks.

\- Effective defensive actions.

\- Successful counterattacks.

\- Appropriate ability usage.

\- Surviving critical situations.



Ultimate abilities must require sufficient Resonance.



\## 7.7. Physical Simulation



The combat system must account for:



\- Angular velocity.

\- Linear velocity.

\- Angular momentum.

\- Collision impulses.

\- Friction.

\- Ground contact.

\- Mass distribution.

\- Stability.

\- Rotational energy loss.

\- Environmental forces.



Physical behavior must be influenced by equipment characteristics.



The system must avoid unrealistic or excessively repetitive collision behavior.



\## 7.8. Victory Conditions



The game must support multiple battle-ending conditions:



| Condition | Description |

|---|---|

| Spin Finish | Opponent loses rotational motion |

| Ring Out | Opponent exits the designated battle area |

| Burst Finish | Opponent's assembly separates |

| Knockout | Opponent receives a decisive impact |

| Survival | Player remains active when the battle timer expires |



Each condition must be configurable per battle mode and arena.

\- Additional customization options.

\- Ability unlocks.

\# 21. Product Constraints and Design Principles



The following constraints apply to all development activities.



1\. The game must remain arena-based.

2\. Combat must remain real-time and physics-driven.

3\. Player control must remain primarily strategic rather than continuous direct movement control.

4\. Spinning tops must be modular and customizable.

5\. Equipment must influence physical behavior.

6\. Combat abilities must be extensible.

7\. Progression must preserve meaningful equipment choices.

8\. Single-player gameplay must function offline.

9\. The architecture must support future multiplayer integration.

10\. Core gameplay systems must not depend on UI implementation.

11\. Game content should be data-driven wherever practical.

12\. New components, abilities, elements, and arenas should be addable without rewriting core systems.

13\. Performance must be considered from the beginning, especially for Android.

14\. Gameplay clarity and responsiveness must take priority over excessive visual effects.

\# 23. Definition of Product Success



Spin Legends will be considered successful when:



\- Players can create meaningfully different spinning top configurations.

\- Physical interactions produce engaging and understandable battles.

\- Tactical decisions influence outcomes.

\- Different combat archetypes provide distinct experiences.

\- Progression creates long-term goals.

\- New equipment and abilities expand strategic possibilities.

\- The game performs reliably on its target platforms.

\- The core gameplay remains enjoyable without requiring multiplayer.



The central success criterion is simple:



\*\*Players should feel that their spinning top is a unique creation, that every battle is influenced by their decisions, and that improving their equipment and mastering its behavior provides a meaningful competitive advantage.\*\*
